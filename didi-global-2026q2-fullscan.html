China converts density into profit; International converts growth into subsidy

Annual active users grew at an 8.9% two-year CAGR, evidence that density is broadening. Yet DiDi does not disclose retention, acquisition cost, incentive-adjusted ARPU, wait time or city-level share. International operated in fourteen countries and concentrated delivery in five Latin American markets. Management states that international mobility itself remained profitable, but it gives no bridge separating that activity from food delivery and local-services losses; the consolidated overseas segment therefore cannot yet be valued as a self-funding growth engine.

Mexico’s digital-platform labor reform took effect in June 2025, while cash payment in Brazil and Mexico adds safety and compliance exposure. The filing provides no country-level revenue, cash contribution, tax, localization, repatriation or organic-versus-acquired growth bridge. These omissions prevent a country DCF and make constant-currency growth a necessary but insufficient quality test.

EARNINGS QUALITY

Reported profit turned positive because the comparison and current adjustments were favorable

The statutory swing is not the economic swing. Q2 2025 carried a large shareholder-lawsuit provision, while the current quarter recognized net income from remeasuring share awards. Removing those items reveals that marketing and overseas incentives, rather than central administration, drove the deterioration in underlying profit.

RULES & CONTROL

Regulation sets the monetization ceiling; governance fixes who controls the response

The historic cybersecurity event is operationally closed: the administrative fine was paid and new-user registration resumed. The live regime is more granular. In May, DiDi cut its stated commission ceiling by two points to 27%, committed to rebates when monthly averages exceed 25%, protected driver pay from rider promotions, prioritized proximity in dispatch and required six hours of rest after ten hours online. These rules improve safety and supply fairness while limiting one path to domestic monetization.

Local permit gap

Shanghai said DiDi was the only operating platform there without a local platform permit; the first enforcement batch covered 153 cases. The exact company fine was not separately disclosed.

Operating compliance

Guangzhou recorded 66 platform violations involving the local branch plus service complaints. Counts are not adjusted for market share and therefore measure friction, not relative quality.

Settlement finality

Cash has entered court escrow, but Q2 materials still condition final effectiveness on court approval and release. Procedural closure remains observable.

Recognized exchange

A formal listing application could improve liquidity and reinstate binding exchange standards. The current filing discloses exploration only, with no assurance or timetable.

FINAL JUDGMENT

A quality domestic platform, priced before the global portfolio proves itself

The base judgment is neutral at the cutoff price: China supplies a defensible profit pool, but the group has not yet demonstrated that its overseas acquisition spending converts into sustainable cash. The strongest counterargument is that food-delivery density can inflect abruptly after scale, leaving today’s losses as the cost of building a larger moat. That argument becomes investable only when the next disclosures show loss contraction without sacrificing activity.

Green cards mark upside confirmation; warning cards define thesis failure.

Overseas repair

By Q4 2026, International quarterly adjusted EBITA loss should fall below RMB2.0bn while constant-currency GTV growth remains above 35%.

Group earnings

Consolidated adjusted EBITA should exceed RMB3bn per quarter without a collapse in China Platform Sales growth.

Cash conversion

Trailing operating cash flow needs to recover above RMB6bn, with treasury liquidity stable above RMB45bn.

Domestic ceiling

The thesis weakens if China EBITA / Platform Sales falls below 15% because incentives or compliance absorb the retained pool.

Runway failure

Cash and treasury investments below RMB40bn, without matching liability reduction or a temporary explanation, forces a lower valuation regime.

Capital leakage

If the fully diluted ADS count stops declining for two consecutive quarters while repurchases continue, gross buyback spend is not creating per-share scarcity.

A recognized-exchange filing would be a genuine catalyst, but only operating evidence can support the next valuation regime. Until overseas losses and cash conversion validate the portfolio, the current quote offers domestic quality without a sufficient discount.
